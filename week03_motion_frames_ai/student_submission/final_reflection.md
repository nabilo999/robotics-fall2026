Respond to any or all of these prompts:

2. How did this activity affect your motivation to do similar kinds of work in the future?
3. What value do you see in connecting technical or computing work with human, ethical, or societal considerations?

## Response

This activity made me more interested in robotics. I liked that the work was not just writing code but also involved predicting the robot’s movement, calculate distances and angles, test the result, and compare it with what actually happened.  

I found the connection between computing and human considerations especially useful. Things like passing distance, stopping time, accessibility, and pedestrian expectations are not problems that can be solved by code alone. They require testing in real environments and thinking about how people actually respond to the system.

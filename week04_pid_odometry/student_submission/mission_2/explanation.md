## Explanations

### calibration

I adjusted the wheel radius and track width while running the calibration movements, comparing the robot’s estimated position to its actual position. I tuned the values until the odometry closely matched the robot’s movement.

### drift

Odometry can still drift because of wheel slippage, uneven surfaces, small measurement errors, and differences in wheel size or encoder readings. These small errors accumulate over time.
